How the muon DIS chain works

There are two chains that share one engine. The vessel chain forces μ-nucleus interactions in the helium, air, PVC walls and veto planes (z = 30.6–82 m). The shield chain forces them in the last 50 cm of MS7 iron and adds a Geant4 transport out of the iron. Both chains then go through the same selection and veto code.

3 · Forced interactions and weights

Why every MC event carries a rate

DIS is rare (P ≈ 10⁻³ for 50 cm of iron, far less in helium), so each interaction is forced and the probability goes into the weight. Draws are importance-sampled per target group (helium, air, PVC, each plane, shield iron).

P_i = n_area · σ(E_i) · 1e-34 m²/μb n_area = nucleons per m² along the chord S_g = Σ_(i in group g) w_i · P_i interactions per spill in group g mode "M draws" : pick crossing i with prob w_i P_i / S_g → weight S_g / M each mode "per track" : k draws on EVERY crossing (draws = −k) → weight w_i P_i / k each per 15 years = weight × 6e20 / 5e13 spills

M draws ∝ w·P (default)

Spends the MC where the rate is. A few high-weight input muons can dominate (the 768.75 class is about 40 % of the shield DIS rate).

One per crossing / track (--v0-copies, draws = −1)

Every parent contributes once. Same central values; the μ errors stay because they come from the few independent input muons (evt_n), not from the number of draws.

  • σ per nucleon of each part = generator σ × the fraction of its events on that side of ν_x. Iron is 0.82 × helium per nucleon (Kokoulin shadowing). The Pythia share is 1.5 % at 10 GeV and 13 % at 100 GeV.
  • Only μ⁻ is generated. With one-photon exchange the hadronic final state is the same for μ⁺, so only the beam-muon sign is flipped.
  • By default K_S, Λ, Σ, Ξ, Ω, π⁰ and η are decayed at generation. That is right in helium but wrong in iron, so the --stable-v0 library keeps K_S and Λ for Geant4 to absorb or decay in flight.

5 · What differs between the two chains

Vessel chain · fastveto.dis.run

  • Targets: helium (code 0), air (1), PVC (2), plane i (10 + i)
  • Muon path A → (K) → B from the cuda-muons store; E interpolated in z
  • Planes: slab of the plane's technology material, thickness / |cos θ| (capped at 0.02)
  • Products are straight lines (no field before the spectrometer)
  • The scattered μ keeps its parent's upstream hits, so the veto can see the parent
  • Entry: bin/run_interactions.py --design D or bin/muon_dis_apply.py

Shield chain · bin/shield_dis.py

  • Target: iron box, codes 3 (interaction) and 4 (V0-decay copy)
  • Straight parents from 29.20 m; parents that stop in the slab are not removed (mean loss only)
  • Geant4 transport of every product through iron and field: absorption and the cascade are included, so no extra e^(−d/λ) weight
  • V0 copies, decaying on a straight line between 30.6 and 80.6 m
  • beam = False: no parent hits upstream
  • prepare → bin/g4.sh … generate --kind muon|nu → run_interactions.py --interactions …

Forced V0 decays (force_v0)

Geant4 stops at z = 29.90 m. A K_L, K_S, Λ or Ξ⁰ that gets out is a neutral particle at that plane; in the original interaction (code 3) it stays undecayed and makes no track. Because a few-GeV K_L has cβγτ of tens of metres, its decay point is not sampled naturally. Instead, with λ = (p/m)cτ and d₁, d₂ the distances along its line to z = 30.6 and 80.6 m:

P_dec = exp(−d₁/λ) − exp(−d₂/λ) (includes survival from the plane to 30.6 m)
  • A copy of the whole interaction (code 4) is made, with the V0 decayed at a point drawn from the exponential truncated to [d₁, d₂], in a random channel.
  • The rest of the event (scattered μ, charged debris) comes along unchanged.
  • Weight of the copy = weight of the interaction × P_dec / N_copies (--v0-copies N only adds statistics).
  • The code-4 weights sum to V0 decays in that z range per spill, not to interactions. The range is a z interval on a straight line; the fiducial cut checks the vessel volume later.

The full weight of a V0-decay copy

For interaction j of muon i, leaving V0 v at the iron exit (force_v0, bin/shield_dis.py:253), in muons per spill:

one draw per track (draws = −k, used for the MS7 numbers): W(j,v) = w_i · P_int,i · P_dec,v / (k · N_copies) M draws ∝ w·P (default), S = Σ_i w_i P_int,i (= 1.04e6 /spill): W(j,v) = (S / M) · P_dec,v / N_copies muon i is picked with probability w_i P_int,i / S, so ⟨picks of i⟩ · S/M = M · (w_i P_int,i / S) · (S/M) = w_i · P_int,i (S/M replaces w_i P_int,i) w_i muon weight from the pkl (muons per spill, max 768.75) P_int,i = n_Fe · σ(E_i) · L_i n_Fe nucleons per m³ of iron (7.874 g/cm³ × N_A ≈ 4.7e30 m⁻³) σ(E_i) library σ per nucleon in iron (Geant4 + Pythia parts) at the vertex energy L_i straight-line length of the muon inside the 50 cm iron box P_dec,v = exp(−d₁/λ_v) − exp(−d₂/λ_v), λ_v = (p_v/m_v)·cτ_v d₁, d₂ = distance from z = 29.90 m to z = 30.6 / 80.6 m along the V0 (Δz / u_z) k forced interactions per muon (k = 1 in the per-track run) N_copies decay copies per V0 (--v0-copies) per 15 years = W × 6e20 / 5e13 = W × 1.2e7

Not weight factors (the Monte Carlo decides them, 0 or 1 per event): whether a V0 exists at the plane at all (library multiplicity, then Geant4 absorption and cascade in the iron, so no extra exp(−d/λ_abs) factor), the decay channel, and the selection. The vessel chain uses the same weight without P_dec: w_i · n_area σ(E_i) / k, with n_area = nucleons per m² along the crossing.

There is no time-coincidence factor because the products of one interaction are in time with each other. Errors come from a 200-sample bootstrap grouped by input muon (evt_n), because rows from the same input muon are not independent. Optional extra cut: daughter asymmetry |A_p|, |A_IP| < a on the same pair (deck chapter 7).

9 · Known limits and open points
  • MS7 K_L is about 2× plain Geant4. Unforced Geant4 gives 0.52 ± 0.15 of the forced chain, and the excess comes from the Pythia (high-ν) component: 9.5 % of interactions but 90 % of the K_L. FairShip uses Geant4 only.
  • FairShip shows a ~17× deficit of shield-made π at SST1 compared with this chain. The muon flux and iron geometry agree, so the cause is still open.
  • Statistics: the MS7 μ number after the full selection rests on a handful of MC events. The next step is K_L-enriched draws.
  • Approximations: straight parents before the shield vertex, no field or scattering in the 0.7 m of air, π/K decays in flight ignored, and n / K_L interactions in the vessel not simulated.
  • Not yet redone with the v0stable library: the DIS×μ combinatorial pairs (dis_comb_*).
