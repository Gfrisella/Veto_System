SHiP veto · combinatorial background · 20 Nov 2026

Why “interaction product × μ” went up

The 20 Nov full-chain runs gave Z2 = 0.041 and Z3 = 0.022 per 15 y after tagging, above R1 (0.019), and R2 without the V0 rule 8× above R2 with it. The cause is in the combinatorial script, not in the designs: it pairs with muons that the SBT-shell model stops before SST1, and it ignores the rest of the interaction that the veto would see.

What the term is

A muon interaction in MS7 that leaves exactly one good track in SST1 is paired with an unrelated muon in the same ±200 ps window. Opposite charge, DOCA, vertex and IP cuts leave 62.7 per 15 y such pairs. The pair is removed if the veto tags either leg.

Finding 1 · the partner muons include muons that never reach SST1

Since 17 Nov R2, R3, Z2 and Z3 use the SBT-shell energy loss. With it, low-momentum muons that come in through the vessel walls stop in the shell. run_reco drops them from the SST1 population; dis_combinatorial does not, so they count as untagged partners. They are only 1.3 % of the simulated rows but 45 % of the weight.

Finding 2 · the rest of the interaction is never used

The script keeps only the product that reaches SST1 and drops the others. In 98 % of the single-track MS7 interactions (by weight) another charged product crosses the 4 × 4 m UBT tile plane, in time with the candidate; 91 % for the Z2/Z3 plane (|x| < 1.7 m). Today none of these hits can tag the pair: only the SST1 track and the partner muon are looked at.

Random coincidences

Rule from now on: random hits only for the LLP signal loss. Background is tagged only by its own hits. New switch RecoConfig.random_hits (UBT planes, SBT, MS7 strips); default unchanged until the background scripts are switched.

Effect on the partner muon: small for R2 and Z2 (+0.9 and +0.2 points untagged), large for R1 (37 → 46 %): 9 points of R1's muon tagging came from random hits.

How the term is computed now (implemented 20 Nov, farm rerun Slurm 2163917)

  1. Partner muons: good SST1 muons (p > 1 GeV, SST1 and timing acceptance); for SBT designs the ones that stop in the shell are removed (710 / spill).
  2. Interactions: MS7 and vessel μ interactions with exactly one good SST1 track; the other charged products (p > 0.1 GeV) stay with it.
  3. Fake vertex: opposite charge, DOCA < 2 cm, vertex 5 cm inside the vessel, measured IP < 10 cm; time coincidence 2 × 200 ps per 1 s spill.
  4. Tagging: the event holds the muon, the SST1 track and the rest of the interaction, each with its own hits on the UBT and SBT. No random hits. The two SST1 tracks are traced back; a hit in the window and in time tags the pair, also a hit of another product. V0 rule as in the design (pvis < 20 GeV).
  5. Signal loss: unchanged, with random hits.
