Muon × rock pairs that survive the tagging

A shield muon and a charged track coming out of the cavern rock (a product of a muon interaction in the wall or floor) cross SST1 within the same 200 ps and fake a vertex in the decay vessel. The shield muon is usually tagged by the UBT. The rock track never crosses the UBT: it enters the vessel through its walls, so only the SBT can tag it.

Same code for all three terms (bin/rock_combinatorial.py): candidate cuts (DOCA < 2 cm, vertex in the vessel), measured IP < 10 cm, then the full tagging (own hits + accidental hits). Rock × rock has no candidate pair at all. "Legs tagged" is the weighted fraction of the selected pairs whose first (shield muon) / second leg is matched to a tagger hit.

Source: bin/rock_combinatorial.py --survivors (job 2090699) on the Nov-4 production (2 M SST1 muons subsampled, 8 547 good tracks / spill) and the rock-muon draws (300 spills, 1.84 × 10⁴ good rock tracks / spill, design-independent).
